01 / Foundation

Research & Engineering Philosophy

I view Machine Learning not as a black box of unpredictable weights, but as a deliberate software engineering discipline built upon probabilistic reasoning, statistical guarantees, and uncompromising data quality.

Throughout my work at scale, I've seen that 80% of modeling failures stem not from model capacity, but from data leakage, unmonitored distributional drift, and brittle serving infrastructure. My methodology couples state-of-the-art transformer and vision backbones with rigorous automated validation pipelines, latency-bounded quantization, and deterministic fallbacks.
